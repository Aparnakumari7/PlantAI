## 📅 **7-Day Treatment Roadmap Example:**

### **Day 1: Immediate Action**
- Remove infected leaves/stems
- Spray with neem oil solution
- Improve drainage if needed

### **Day 2: Follow-up**
- Check for new symptoms
- Apply copper fungicide if spreading
- Reduce watering frequency

### **Day 3: Monitoring**
- Inspect plant thoroughly
- Switch to chemical treatment if needed
- Ensure good air circulation

### **Day 4: Assessment**
- Evaluate treatment effectiveness
- Apply systemic fungicide if worsening
- Continue current treatment if improving

### **Day 5: Maintenance**
- Continue monitoring
- Apply second treatment round
- Remove new infected tissue

### **Day 6: Progress Check**
- Document improvement/decline
- Adjust treatment intensity
- Consider soil treatment if needed

### **Day 7: Weekly Review**
- Assess overall plant health
- Plan next week's schedule
- Switch treatments if no improvement
